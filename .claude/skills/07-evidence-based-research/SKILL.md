# Evidence-Based Technical Research

## Objective

Base technical decisions on reliable, relevant, and sufficiently up-to-date information.

## Procedure

1. Define what you need to find out and why it affects the project.
2. Identify the most appropriate sources to resolve the issue.
3. Prioritize official documentation, specifications, and primary sources.
4. Consult additional sources when necessary to verify claims or evaluate alternatives.
5. Verify relevant versions, compatibility, requirements, and limitations.
6. Distinguish between verified information and inferences.
7. Summarize the findings and explain how they affect the decision.
8. Recommend an alternative only when there is sufficient evidence to justify it.

## Evaluation

Consider, as appropriate:

- Maturity and maintenance.
- Compatibility with other technologies.
- Security and known risks.
- Costs and licensing restrictions.
- Operational complexity.
- Performance and scalability.
- Availability of documentation and support.
- Ease of migration and maintenance.

## Rules

- Do not choose a technology based on popularity.
- Do not confuse advertising with technical evidence.
- Do not assume. Verify.

## Expected Outcome

A recommendation based on identifiable sources, explicit criteria, relevant alternatives, and known limitations.
